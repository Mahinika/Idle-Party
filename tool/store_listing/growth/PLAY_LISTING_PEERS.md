## What charts actually do (per surface)

### Title (30 chars)

Winners put **idle RPG** (or AFK RPG) in the indexed name. Melvor and MapleStory
do this. RAID/AFK Arena skip it because the brand already ranks.

**Idle Party:** paste `Idle Party: Idle RPG` (20 chars). Live page still showed
bare **Idle Party** on 2026-09-17.

### Short description (80)

One sentence: genre + one benefit. No CTA, no “#1”, no price.

- Melvor: what the game *is* (idle/incremental + inspiration).
- RAID/AFK: collection count or login gift — **policy + fairness: skip**.

**Idle Party:** keep current short (idle RPG + party AFK + TODAY).

### Full description

Hook paragraph → ALL-CAPS or bold sections → systems → IAP honesty.

Gacha pages lead with summons. Honest idle (Melvor) leads with the loop.
Idle Party already matches Melvor’s shape. Polish: first lines must say you
**see the crawl** (party on camera), not only “idle numbers”.

### Category / tags

Stay in **Role Playing**. Play tags are a closed list, max 5.
Idle Party live: **Clicker-rollspel** + **Rollspel** (SV Console). No fake
Idle/Incremental tag. Never Casual / Action / Arcade / Rogue-liknande.

Keywords belong in title/short/full: idle RPG, AFK, party, dungeon, Ascend.

### Screenshots (first 2–3 = search)

Play search shows 2–3 thumbs. Games that convert:

1. Core loop in frame 1 (auto-battle / party fight), not a menu.
2. Huge caption (readable at ~25% zoom).
3. Story: fight → progress-away → build → map.

Gacha uses splash + “1000 summons”. We use **new-save Sandy combat**, party
centered, English captions ≤8 words, 1080×1920, ≥3 qualifying 9:16 shots
(Play featuring).

### Feature graphic (1024×500)

One idea + title, important bits in the center (crop/video overlay).
Idle Party: party + title; caption promise = **party fights AFK**.

### Preview video (15–30 s, YouTube)

Play Help: gameplay in the **first 10 s**, ≥80% real UI, mute autoplay, ads
off, not age-restricted. Charts cut to combat immediately.

**Idle Party gap:** old brief opened on hub TODAY. New brief opens on live
combat, then AFK / TODAY / GEAR / title.

## Idle Party do / don’t

**Do**

- Genre in title; short = one true benefit.
- Shot 1–2 = visible crawl (camera on the party).
- Preview: combat first 10 s.
- Fair SHOP / no gacha line in full desc.

**Don’t**

- “#1”, Best of Play, 10% off, free summons, champion counts as the hook.
- Fake chrome or other-game footage.
- KEY/Gauntlet as search-lead screenshots.
- Extra Play locales until the owner says paste (`LOCALES.md`).
