# Idle Party VFX v3: inspiration → technique → how it was applied

This is a concept pass only. Nothing in the repo was changed. No art was copied: every effect is procedural and was drawn from scratch with `px3.py` / `effects3.py`. The sources below were used for *principles* only.

## Sources and techniques

| # | Technique | Source (URL) | How it was applied in v3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **No dark outline on light sources.** The outermost ring is a dark *fire/magic* colour; the brown game outline is kept only for solid objects | Pixeli "VFX: fire, smoke, explosions, magic": https://pixeli.pebbly.space/knowledge/rules/80-vfx-fire-smoke-magic · Pixor "Pixel art glow": https://pixor.art/learn/pixel-art-glow/ | `F.glow()` shades emissive shapes rim→core with the school ramp and no #341a10. `solid_shade()+outline()` is used only for hammer, shield, arrow, skulls, ice shards and leaves |
| 2 | **Hot-core → cool-edge ramps**: white for only 1–3 px at the core, the hue shifts toward yellow/white at the core and toward the darker, cooler hue at the rim | Pixeli r80 (fire ramp, projectile cores warm/borders cool): same URL · Pixor glow | Each school has a new 5-tone ramp in `px3.R` (rim→mid→light→bright→hot). `glow(hot=1..3)` places only 1–3 HOT pixels at the deepest point |
| 3 | **Ring glow without blur; dithered outer ring** | Pixor glow: https://pixor.art/learn/pixel-art-glow/ · Pixeli "Dithering and texture": https://pixeli.pebbly.space/knowledge/rules/13-dithering-and-texture | `dither_light()` lights the *floor* under each bolt with 2 flat rings, the outer ring Bayer-4×4 dithered. This replaces the additive `_SoftGlow` blur disc (light spill onto neighbours, still pixel-crisp) |
| 4 | **Smear frame on launch**: 1 frame, opaque, using the two brightest colours | Pixeli "Attacks and impacts": https://pixeli.pebbly.space/knowledge/rules/44-attacks-and-impacts · Saint11 Motion Blur / Smear: https://saint11.art/blog/pixel-art-tutorials/ | `smear()`: the first flight frame is a stretched tear in ramp[3]/ramp[4], shown for 50 ms |
| 5 | **Muzzle flash** as a 4-point star / first frame | Pixeli "Impacts and game feel": https://pixeli.pebbly.space/knowledge/rules/81-impacts-and-game-feel · Vlambeer "Art of Screenshake": https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AJdEqssNZ-U (notes: https://www.bluetengu.com/2014/12/12/art-of-screenshake-experiments/) | `muzzle()` draws a school-coloured 4-point star at the hand on the launch frame |
| 6 | **Anticipation / charge**: gather in, then release flash | Saint11 tutorials (anticipation, Impact): https://saint11.art/blog/pixel-art-tutorials/ · Dead Cells, pose-to-pose with VFX carrying the motion: https://www.gamedeveloper.com/production/art-design-deep-dive-using-a-3d-pipeline-for-2d-animation-in-i-dead-cells-i- | `charge()`: 3 frames of school particles converge (leaves, darts, sparks, embers) around a growing school glyph, then a 1-frame release star. Timing is 90/80/70/**40** ms |
| 7 | **Impact order**: contrast/white flash → sharp blast → body → fade, fast out then slow | Pixeli r80 (explosion order) and r81 (contrast frame, then a 3-frame spark: compact → rays → dots) · Slynyrd Pixelblog 31 (white-hot → warm → grey smoke): https://www.slynyrd.com/blog/2020/12/14/pixelblog-31-shmup-sprite-design | `impact()` has 5 frames: **f0** white school-shaped star (4-arm, or 6-arm for frost/storm), **f1** sharp angular blast, **f2** school body (fire lobes, ice shards, skull, cross, leaves, splat, rune ring), **f3** fade, **f4** debris landed. Durations 45/70/80/90/110 ms (fast → slow) |
| 8 | **Hit flash on the target + hitstop + knockback** | Pixeli r44 (hitstop 3/6/9 frames, 1–2 white frames) · Slynyrd Pixelblog 36 (flash frames): https://www.slynyrd.com/blog/2022/5/24/pixelblog-38-metroid-study & https://www.slynyrd.com/blog/2022/1/25/pixelblog-36-8-bit-adventure · Vlambeer (hit flash, knockback, ~20 ms sleep) · Dead Cells hitstop on crits: https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/designing-each-of-the-50-weapons-in-i-dead-cells-i-to-feel-distinctive | In the GIFs the enemy turns into a flat white silhouette on impact f0 and is pushed 3→2→1→0 px. The blast frame is held longer (hitstop feel). The pyroblast/chaos (big) impact is 1.3× larger. "Biggest feedback for about 1 in 10 events" (Pixeli r81) → keep big impacts for big spells and crits |
| 9 | **Secondary debris with gravity** | Pixeli r81 (spark → dots) · Slynyrd 31 (blobs/debris) | `impact()` debris follows a parabola and lands on the ground line (y=50): embers, ice shards that tumble and land, leaves that sway down, poison droplets, dust puffs for the hammer |
| 10 | **Permanence: marks left on the ground that fade** | Vlambeer "Art of Screenshake" (permanence): https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AJdEqssNZ-U · Noita trail/ground fx: https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Fire_Trail | `ground()` decals: scorch, fel scorch, lava pool, frost patch with cracks, slime puddle, shadow stain, holy glyph, arcane rune, petals, hammer crack. They fade in 3 steps **by removing pixels through an ordered dither**, not with alpha |
| 11 | **Smoke dies by shrinking/breaking up; violet-grey, not black** | Pixeli r80 · Saint11 Smoke/Explosions: https://saint11.art/blog/pixel-art-tutorials/ | New `smoke` ramp (violet-grey). The fire/shadow fade frames shrink into 2–3 clusters that rise |
| 12 | **Uneven frame durations for fire flicker** | Pixeli r80 (6–8 frame loops, uneven) | Fire flight frames: every third frame +15 ms. The flame body edge and tongue lengths change per frame (`J()` jitter) |
| 13 | **Electricity flickers with blank frames** | Pixeli r80 | `beam_lightning()` cycles shock → bright (with forks) → **blank** → echo → **blank**. In `beam_chain()` each hop flickers in turn. The target flash appears only on the bright frame |
| 14 | **Beam = white core + coloured fringe, thickness pulse 1-3-5-3, charge first** | Pixeli r80 | `beam_wave()` (drain life, mind flay) pulses 1-3-5-3. Moonfire/Smite pillars pulse 1-3-5-7-5-3 with a 1 px white core and charge-ups first |
| 15 | **Dark magic = complementary pair, rim-lit; holy = analogous, round, sparks rising slowly, no smoke** | Pixeli r80 | Shadow bolt uses an **inverted glow** (dark heart, bright violet rim) and a spiral wisp. Chaos bolt has a black heart with a lime rim. Holy is gold→white with rounded cross shapes, sparks drift up slowly and there is no smoke |
| 16 | **Projectiles need varied shapes ("more than simple balls") and high contrast; double bright+dark outline for readability** | Slynyrd Pixelblog 31: https://www.slynyrd.com/blog/2020/12/14/pixelblog-31-shmup-sprite-design · Enter the Gungeon bullets (white core + red rim, enlarged twice for visibility): https://enterthegungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Projectiles · https://www.reddit.com/r/EnterTheGungeon/comments/4v1nx6/how_etgs_bullets_evolved/ | Each school has its own silhouette (flame tear, ice blade, rim-lit orb + wisp, diamond darts, lance + rays, skull comet, spinning leaves, hammer, shield, star). **1.2× visual scale** over v2 (fireball ≈ 36×15 art px ≈ 10×4 dp). The ETG idea also drives hero-cue option B |
| 17 | **Fast objects: fewer details, stretched along travel; don't rotate pixel art for spins, use smears** | Pixeli r81 | Frostbolt is stretched 1.15× along travel and ice lance is a long thin spear with dashed speed pixels. Arrows get speed dashes. The hammer spins with alternating smear-arc frames instead of a rotated sprite, and the shield "spins" with squash (12→8→3→8 px wide) |
| 18 | **Saturated neon effects over muted environments** | Hyper Light Drifter: https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/road-to-the-igf-heart-machine-s-i-hyper-light-drifter-i- | The dungeon tiles stay untouched (muted grey). Only effect cores carry full saturation, so bolts pop at A56 size |
| 19 | **Trail emitters along the path** | Noita (trail emitters): https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Fire_Trail · https://gdcvault.com/play/1025695/Exploring-the-Tech-of-Noita | Fire sheds detached embers behind it. The arcane darts leave a 7-dot wavy sparkle trail, death coil and shadow have tapering wisps, the starfire trail is a violet streak |
| 20 | **Per-school motion as an identity cue** | Own design decision (Wizard of Legend, Children of Morta, Octopath and the mobile idle RPGs were not checked this pass); grounded in Pixeli r81 (fast = stretched, motion carries the read): https://pixeli.pebbly.space/knowledge/rules/81-impacts-and-game-feel | GIF motion paths: fire **wobble**; frost **straight and fast** (6 frames, 45 ms); shadow **sine**; arcane **homing curves** (3 staggered darts on Bézier curves); nature **spin** + slight arc; holy/star/hammer **from above**, a steep vertical descent; serpent/lava **lob** |
| 21 | **Clutter control / accessibility** | Brotato (projectile opacity, explosion flash toggle): https://teemo.dev/game-design/brotato/systems/ux-ui-and-accessibility/ · Vampire Survivors (disable flashing VFX): https://vampire.survivors.wiki/w/Options | Proposal: the white-flash frames and target white-silhouette should obey a "reduce flashing" setting (Lite → swap the white for ramp[3]). Decals are capped (the ground pool is already 6) |
| 22 | **Asset-pack conventions (sizes/frame counts)** | itch.io packs, e.g. https://cancapstudio.itch.io/elemental-spells-auras (32×32 projectiles × 6 frames, 64×64 impacts × 8), https://stonebit-studio.itch.io/spell-combat-fx-3232-pixel-art-animated | Used as a sanity check for budgets only: v3 uses 4-frame projectile loops and 5-frame impacts at ≤ 64 px art, which is in line with commercial packs, and nothing was copied |

## Hero-bolt cue (replacing the PR53 ">" chevron), see `hero_cue_options.png`
- **A (today):** a white ">" chevron on top of every hero bolt. It reads as UI clutter and fights the bolt shape.
- **B (recommended):** hero bolts are identified by their **school silhouette** alone. Enemy bolts become one uniform **round shot with a white core and red rim** (Enter the Gungeon logic). Allegiance is then carried by *shape* (school shapes vs uniform round), so it stays colour-blind safe, which was PR53's goal.
- **C (optional add-on):** a 1 px **gold rim on the leading half** of hero bolts (`F.rim(gold, side='lead')`). It is subtle, but it helps when hero and enemy share a school (for example fire mages vs fire hero).

## Engine-side notes (if this is implemented later, not done now)
- All changes are visual-only fields. `p.radius` and speed are combat values and stay untouched.
- Caps stay the same (projectiles 36, bursts 16, ground 6, sparks 140/50). Debris can reuse the spark pool. Decals reuse the ground-FX pool with a 3-step dither fade.
- Smear, flash and hitstop are 1–2 frame swaps; no per-frame alpha is needed.
