### A-grade claims that could reorder Nu
- GameAnalytics 2025: median D7 is only ~3.4–3.9% and most games stay under 3% D28—market context, not Idle Party KPIs.
- GameAnalytics 2026: 2025 median session stayed 3.1–3.5 minutes—context for a short Nu, not a timer lock.
- Play listing experiments: change one asset at a time and run ≥1 week; tiny listing traffic means A/B is not ready by default.
- Preview assets: show real gameplay; core UX in first ~10 seconds; aim for ~80% real experience vs logos/cutscenes.
- YouTube on the Play listing: public, embeddable, ads off; a games clip on the listing needs an upload within 90 days.
- Store-listing best practices: no ranking/price badges; keep art readable at phone scale.
- Android vitals: stay under ~1.09% user-perceived crash and ~0.47% user-perceived ANR or Play can cut discoverability.
- Frommel CHI PLAY: daily quests/login rewards can motivate or become FOMO/chores—do not stack pest calendars into Nu.
- Pecorella idle math: prestige when next reset is roughly +50–200% prestige currency; use log/fractional growth and bumpy cycles.
- Pecorella Parts I–III: keep generators relevant across prestiges; derivative chains and reset loops are the idle clock backbone.
- Play icon + technical-quality docs: shipping polish includes asset compliance and core vitals, not invented Console % lifts.
