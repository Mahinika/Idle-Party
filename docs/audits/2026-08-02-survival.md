## Summary

Survival has the right **name plate** (Explosive Shot, Serpent Sting, Explosive/Freezing Trap, Black Arrow, Disengage) and mid Prot+Disc share is fair (~51%). Under the hood most “DoTs/traps” are **instant damage / self-nova / root**, Disengage is a **shield emergency** (not a kite), and **Mongoose Bite** fires as a full-range arrow. Trap Mastery only buffs `kitOutMul` + root bonus. Verdict: **tune** (identity soft, not dead).
