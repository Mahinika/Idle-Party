## Summary

Blood’s **fantasy is self-healing plate tank**, but Idle wiring undercuts that: **Death Strike / Rune Tap are party-triage `heal`** (no strike damage), and **Heart Strike is `aoe`-gated**, so on single non-elite trash the kit often has **no damage filler**—only white hits + taunt. Presence / VB / IBF / DRW / Blood Boil still read DK-ish. Verdict: **WIP** until ST self-heal strike path exists.

#### 10. Findings

- **P0:**
  1. **No working ST damage builder** — Heart Strike AoE-gated; Death Strike is `heal` with **no damage** and party triage. On single non-elite trash, kit damage fillers do not fire.
- **P1:**
  1. Retarget Death Strike (and ideally Rune Tap) to **self-heal ± damage**, not healer `_lowestAlly` path.
  2. Add a disease / maintain beat (even a single DoT or “plague” flag) for Blood identity.
  3. Consider Heart Strike as `damage` (optional mild cleave) so ST always has a builder.
- **P2:**
  1. Death Grip-lite pull or accept Dark Command-only.
  2. Party buff crumb (Hysteria-lite).
  3. Offline + ST combat tests; VFX tint.
