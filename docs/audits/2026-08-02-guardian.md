## Summary

Guardian is a **solid bear kit skeleton**: Bear Form passive, Mangle / Maul ST, Swipe AoE, Barkskin / Survival Instincts, Growl, Frenzied Regen, Berserk. Biggest gaps: **no Lacerate maintain** (core Wrath bear beat) and **Berserk is a rage grant** rather than an offensive window; Frenzied Regen uses party-triage `heal`. Still recognizable enough to **tune**, not WIP.
