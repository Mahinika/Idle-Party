## Summary

Druid kits are **functionally shippable** — abilities cast, roles/ranges look right, live DPS share sits in-band (Balance ~58%, Feral ~52%). The class **fails the form-identity contract**: all four specs are tinted humanoid paper-dolls (mage/rogue/warrior/healer bodies), while passives claim always-on Moonkin / Cat / Bear / Tree. That is the single biggest gap. Secondary: dead Eclipse mastery, Feral combo HUD with no combo engine, Guardian copy says “Thrash” but kit has Swipe.
