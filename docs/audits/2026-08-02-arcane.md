#### 9. Findings

- **P0:** Mid DPS share HIGH (~66%) — **systemic caster ceiling** (selfBuff sets both `powerInfusionTimer` ×1.4 and `buffTimers['haste']` ×1.28 on whites)
- **P1:** Wire Ice Block to `iceBlockTimer` (match Fire) or rename emergency
- **P1:** Passive copy “Brilliance” vs Arcane Power naming clash; party AI still personal-only
- **P2:** Optional Missile Barrage lite proc
