# Stats cap audit — all classes — 2026-08-20

**Shipped in 1.12.23:** healer loot Mp5/Crit first; BiS Crit fades at sheet 70–75.

**Depth:** full (code + live sheet numbers from a full-set probe; no Wowhead number scrape)  
**Playtest:** no  
**Scope:** secondary caps (crit / haste / lifesteal), combat crit clamp, Forge %, kit haste stacking. All 31 `HeroSpecId`.  
**Not in scope:** kit rotations, VFX, hit/expertise tables (still intentionally absent).

WotLK is used for **shape** only (what a cap is *for*). Idle numbers stay Idle.

## Verdict

Caps are **the same numbers for every class**, but they **do not land the same**.

Crit and haste only speed and punch **white swings**. Kit spells/heals ignore both. Late rare sets already slam AGI-family into the 75% crit wall, and slam healers into the 50% haste wall — while Auto Equip still chases those stats.

**Open P0:** 0 (combat still runs; nobody soft-locks).  
**Open P1:** 2 (AGI crit saturation; healer haste is mostly dead).  
**Open P2:** 2 (mage base APS; 4pc haste on already-capped leather).

## What the caps actually are

| Layer | Crit | Haste | Lifesteal |
|-------|------|-------|-----------|
| Gear soft → hard | 18 → 40 | 22 → 50 | 10 → 22 |
| Curve past soft | keep 45% of overflow | same | same |
| Forge | soft at **25** (then ×0.35) | soft at **40** | — |
| Combat | `(sheet + forge).clamp(0, 75)` | no extra clamp; kit muls stack | whites only |
| Kit spells / heals | **do not roll crit** | **CDs tick wall-clock** | **whites only** |

Source: `PartyHero._softCapStat`, `GameState.effectiveHeroCrit` / `effectiveHeroAttackSpeed` / `softForgePercent`, `SpatialActor.attackSpeedMul`.

White swing rate:

```
APS = roleBase × (1 + (cappedGearHaste + forgeHaste) / 100)
then fireCooldown drains × kitHaste × SnD × Killing Spree × PI
```

Role base APS (before gear): mage **1.82** · rogue **1.55** · healer **1.43** · warrior **1.35**.

## Keep (working as designed)

| Piece | Why |
|-------|-----|
| Same gear soft/hard for every spec | Phone-readable; no hidden per-class rating tables |
| Crit combat wall 75% | Anti-explode; matches 2026-08-17 attributes note |
| Forge % soft-cap | Infinite gold buys stay useful without breaking combat |
| No hit / expertise / dodge caps | First hour stays calm |
| Move not on loot | `gearMoveSpeedBonus` has no cap — loot already rolls 0 |
| Lifesteal 10/22 | Only an on-hit effect; whites only |

## P1 — AGI family sits on the 75% crit wall

Late leather/mail sets show **sheet 79–91**, then combat clamps to 75. Extra Crit on loot, 4pc +2 crit, and Auto Equip Crit weight (7–8) are **ghost power**.

Feral is the loudest (sheet ~91). Hunters/rogues/Enh are the same shape.

**Fix shape (if we take this):** after sheet crit would exceed ~70, BiS should stop paying for Crit (or loot should prefer Haste/AP/Sta). Do not copy WoW rating conversions. Keep the 75 combat wall.

## P1 — Healer haste cap is a dead stat

Healer loot always fills Haste first. Late sets sit on **haste 50**. Ability CDs and heals **do not haste**. Crit almost never rolls (~0–2). The useful secondary they actually get is Mp5.

Holy/Disc/Resto look “haste capped” on the sheet and feel nothing in the kit.

**Fix shape:** healer secondary pool `mp5, crit, haste` (Mp5 first), or haste kit heals/CDs by a small amount. Prefer pool swap — don’t invent a Wrath GCD table.

## P2 — Mage whites are already the fastest

Same 50% haste on a 1.82 base outruns plate 1.35 by ~35% before kit buffs. Fine if casters are meant to plink; it feeds the old “caster white ceiling” when PI also multiplies whites.

## P2 — 4pc haste on leather after the wall

`setRoleHasteBonus` +2 for rogue affinity only helps before the 50 hard cap. On late sets it is usually eaten by `_softCapStat`.

## Also noted (not P1)

- **Lifesteal 22** lights up on plate/AGI; casters roll 0 (effect pool). Fair for melee identity.
- **Tank haste-first loot** caps haste while Crit still has room — tanks don’t need crit.
- **Ability crit** does not exist. A Fire Mage at 58% sheet crit only crits **wands/whites**, not Fireball.
- Do **not** add hit-cap, expertise, or dodge DR. That would fight the first-hour calm lock.

## Recommended order

1. **Healer loot pool** — cheapest honesty; Mp5/crit before haste.  
2. **AGI Crit after ~70 sheet** — stop BiS chasing a clamped stat (Feral/hunters/rogues/Enh).  
3. Leave plate/caster caps as-is unless share-fast says whites are the problem.

Do **not** make per-class hard caps (rogue 60 / mage 75). One wall, honest loot, is enough.
