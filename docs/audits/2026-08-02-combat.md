## Summary

Combat is a **recognizable leather melee kit** (SnD, Blade Flurry, Killing Spree, Vanish, Sprint, combo HUD) with fair mid DPS share (~49%). The biggest gap is **combo priority AI**: Kidney Shot spends at 3 CP and permanently blocks Eviscerate (≥4), and short mid clears often end before SnD ever arms. Verdict: **tune**.

#### 10. Findings

- **P0** (wire / role / must-bucket / art / soft-lock):
  1. **Combo priority:** Kidney Shot spends at ≥3 CP ahead of SnD refresh and permanently blocks Eviscerate (≥4). Reorder: maintain SnD first; Kidney only when SnD safe (or without dumping CP needed for Evis); allow Evis dump at 4–5.
- **P1** (identity / AI / gear / VFX / unlock UX / modes):
  1. **Adrenaline Rush**-style energy CD missing (Combat signature alongside Spree).
  2. **Short FARM ramp:** SnD often never applies on mid clears that die before 3 CP — consider lower SnD CP gate (1–2) or CP on first swing / opener assist.
  3. Catalog vs ticker: Flurry/Spree `AbilityEffectKind` unused by legacy path (doc/clarity only).
- **P2** (polish / curve / copy / timing / perf):
  1. Optional party util (Tricks fantasy) — skip unless composition needs a buff niche.
  2. Guide blurb: call out SnD + Flurry + Spree as the readable loop.
