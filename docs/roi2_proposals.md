# ROI2 design drafts (not in the game)

Nothing here is chargeable, flagged on, or saved. Prices are proposals for Robert.

## R2-047 Starter pack after the first boss

Window: 48 hours on the hub, starting when the first boss dies. One purchase. Hidden after the window or after buy.

Contents (proposal):

- 6 hours Scroll of Battle (same ×2 gold and ATK as a ticket, still under the 24h cap)
- 2 Ad Tickets
- 500 gold

Price proposal: the same shelf as a small consumable, about the cost of two rewarded sessions of value. Not a new SKU until Robert picks a Play price. Do not reuse `ad_free`.

## R2-124 Monthly pass, designed with the VIP pass

VIP pass (catalog id `vip_pass`, flag `vip_pass_listed`, default off, not chargeable): 5 tickets a day, lantern gold on tap, Welcome Back ×3, 5 CAMP levels a day, free quest rerolls.

Monthly pass proposal, sold beside VIP, not instead of it:

- 30 days
- The VIP daily tickets only (5 a day) for that month
- One extra Daily Vault claim bonus of +10 essence, not a change to the vault formula
- No gold multiplier and no gear
- Does not include Ad-free. Ad-free stays the permanent skip-ads product
- Price: TBD. Do not show it until a Play product exists

Existing Ad-free buyers keep VIP perks (grandfather). A new Ad-free purchase does not include this pass.

## R2-125 After AL20

Ascend stops at AL20. Reborn already wipes the bag and keeps heroes and Apex.

Proposal: each Reborn grants 1 Paragon point. Spend is a small tree next to Blessing, cap 20 points. Each point is +1% gold find or +1% essence, player's choice, one point per Reborn. No new combat rating. Kept on later Reborns. Not built.
